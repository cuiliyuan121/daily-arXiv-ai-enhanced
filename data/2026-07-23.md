### [1] [VQ-Transplant: Efficient VQ-Module Integration for Pre-trained Visual Tokenizers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19575)
*Xianghong Fang,Yuan Yuan,Dehan Kong,Tim G. J. Rudner*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：提出VQ-Transplant框架，通过将新的VQ模块插入冻结的预训练分词器，无需昂贵端到端重训练即可实现高效量化集成，降低95%训练成本并保持近SOTA重建性能。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有VQ模型训练量化模块需要大量计算资源，资源受限条件下难以开发新型VQ技术。

方法：VQ-Transplant：将新VQ模块即插即用替换冻结的预训练分词器中的原生VQ模块，保持编解码器参数不变；辅以轻量级解码器适配策略（仅需ImageNet-1k上5轮训练）以缓解解码器与量化空间不匹配问题。

结果：在VAR等工业级模型上达到近SOTA重建保真度，训练成本降低95%。

结论：VQ-Transplant通过资源高效的方式集成新型VQ技术，同时匹配工业级重建性能，推动了量化研究的民主化。

原始摘要：Vector Quantization (VQ) underpins modern discrete visual tokenization. However, training quantization modules for state-of-the-art VQ-based models requires significant computational resources which, in practice, all but prevents the development of novel, cutting-edge VQ techniques under resource constraints. To address this limitation, we propose {\bf VQ-Transplant}, a simple framework that enables plug-and-play integration of new VQ modules into frozen, pre-trained tokenizers by replacing their native VQ modules. Crucially, the proposed transplantation process preserves all encoder-decoder parameters, obviating the need for costly end-to-end retraining when modifying the quantization method. To mitigate decoder-quantization mismatch, we introduce a lightweight decoder adaptation strategy (trained for only 5 epochs on ImageNet-1k) to align feature priors with the new quantization space. In our empirical evaluation, we find that VQ-Transplant allows obtaining near state-of-the-art reconstruction fidelity for industry-level models like VAR while reducing the training cost by 95\%. VQ-Transplant democratizes quantization research by enabling resource-efficient integration of novel VQ techniques while matching industry-level reconstruction performance.

### [2] [OSVE: One Step Video Editing with One Step Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19895)
*Habin Lim,Gyeong-Moon Park*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出OSVE框架，首次成功将单步文本到图像模型适配于高质量视频编辑，通过可学习编码器预测噪声、结构感知编辑损失保持几何一致性、以及统一帧编辑技术实现时间连贯性，在达到或超越现有多步方法编辑质量的同时，速度快155-171倍。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有基于扩散模型的文本引导视频编辑方法因多步采样和反演过程而速度极慢，难以满足实际实时应用需求。

方法：1) 训练可学习编码器，单次前向预测每帧初始噪声，替代缓慢的迭代反演；2) 提出结构感知编辑(SAE)损失，在结构对齐的图像对数据集上训练，保留源视频几何结构；3) 提出统一帧编辑(UFE)技术，拼接帧潜变量实现跨帧注意力；4) 长视频采用滑动窗口+锚帧策略维持全局一致性。

结果：OSVE在编辑质量上与最先进的多步方法相当或更优，运行速度快155-171倍。

结论：该工作为实用化、实时的视频编辑应用铺平了道路。

原始摘要：Text-guided video editing with diffusion models is impractically slow, hindered by costly multi-step sampling and inversion. We present OSVE, the first framework to successfully adapt one-step Text-to-Image (T2I) models for high-quality video editing, addressing the core challenges of inversion, editability, and temporal consistency. To bypass slow iterative inversion, we train a learnable encoder that predicts the initial noise for each frame in a single forward pass. This encoder is trained with a novel Structure-Aware Editing (SAE) loss on a curated dataset of structurally-aligned image pairs, teaching it to preserve the source video's geometry during edits. For temporal coherence, we introduce Unified-Frame Editing (UFE), a technique that concatenates frame latents to facilitate cross-frame attention in a single generation step. Furthermore, for long videos, a sliding-window strategy with an anchor frame maintains global consistency. Our extensive experiments demonstrate that OSVE achieves editing quality comparable or superior to state-of-the-art multi-step methods, while operating approximately 155--171 times faster. This breakthrough paves the way for practical, real-time video editing applications. Code is available at https://github.com/KU-VGI/OSVE.

### [3] [HeadCast: Casting Attention Heads for Efficient Autoregressive Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20125)
*Jinliang Shen,Lianghao Su,Zheming Li,Kang He,ZiLiang Lai,Yanbing Jiang,Chengru Song*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：HeadCast是一种无需训练、即插即用的加速框架，通过将自回归视频扩散模型中的注意力头分类为四种原型（Sink、Dummy、Spatial、Global），并为每种头设计专用KV缓存路径，在保持视频质量的同时实现最高1.95倍加速。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自回归视频扩散模型中不断增长的KV缓存导致注意力计算成为主要的推理开销，尤其是高分辨率下每帧贡献大量token，现有方法要么使用粗糙启发式策略导致帧间闪烁，要么需要重新训练模型。

方法：在短时预热后，在最大噪声步骤进行一次分类，将每个注意力头划分为四种原型之一：Sink、Dummy、Spatial、Global，并将统一的KV缓存重构为头专用的路径。其中Global头被保留以维护长程时间一致性，Spatial路径基于固定大小网格操作。

结果：在多个最先进的自回归模型上，HeadCast在720P分辨率下实现1.62倍加速，1080P下实现1.95倍加速，同时VBench质量与全注意力持平且基本无闪烁。

结论：HeadCast是一种有效的训练免费加速方案，通过注意力头的异构行为分类实现高分辨率视频生成加速，且不会牺牲质量。

原始摘要：Autoregressive (AR) video diffusion models have become a promising paradigm for long and streaming video synthesis, but the continuously growing Key-Value (KV) cache makes attention the dominant inference cost, especially at high resolution where each frame contributes many tokens. Existing remedies either evict the cache with coarse heuristics that cause inter-frame flickering, or require model re-training. We propose HeadCast, a training-free, plug-and-play acceleration framework built on the observation that a pre-trained AR model's attention heads exhibit stable, heterogeneous behaviors. After a short warm-up, HeadCast performs a one-time classification at the maximum-noise step that sorts every head into one of four archetypes: Sink, Dummy, Spatial, and Global, and restructures the monolithic KV cache into head-specific pathways. Crucially, it retains the Global heads that preserve the long-range temporal consistency aggressive eviction destroys. Because the Spatial pathway operates on a fixed-size grid, its savings grow with resolution: across state-of-the-art AR models, HeadCast accelerates inference by up to 1.62x at 720P and 1.95x at 1080P, while keeping VBench quality on par with full attention and largely flicker-free. Code is available at https://github.com/sjlgaga/HeadCast .

</details>

We further introduce a memory distance scaling module to strengthen long-range access to early interaction states. Extensive comparisons with both long-video baselines and recent HOI generation methods demonstrate that StreamHOI achieves strong interaction plausibility, object fidelity, human quality and efficiency, reaching 17.6 FPS with 0.75s first-chunk latency.

### [6] [Self Gradient Forcing: Native Long Video Extrapolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20368)
*Junhao Zhuang,Shiyi Zhang,Yuxuan Bian,Yaowei Li,Yawen Luo,Yijun Liu,Weiyang Jin,Songchun Zhang,Xianglong He,Xuying Zhang,Haoran Li,Haoyang Huang,Zeyue Xue,Nan Duan*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出Self Gradient Forcing (SGF)，一种双通道训练策略，通过恢复历史上下文梯度信号来解决自回归视频扩散中的上下文-梯度差距问题，仅用5秒训练窗口即可生成数分钟的长视频，在主体一致性、背景/布局一致性和时间稳定性上优于Self Forcing。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有自回归视频扩散方法基于Self Forcing训练，但历史KV缓存仅作为冻结的 rollout 状态供未来帧使用，导致未来损失无法监督早期生成潜变量如何编码为更有用的键值对，即历史上下文-梯度差距问题。

方法：提出SGF双通道训练策略：通道1执行无梯度自回归rollout模拟推理，在采样去噪退出步记录自生成上下文和噪声潜变量；通道2对记录的退出步执行并行上下文梯度重建，使用自生成上下文作为停止梯度的干净潜变量输入，让模型重新计算上下文KV表示和未来到上下文的因果注意力，从而提供缺失的记忆写入监督信号。

结果：在多种长程帧级和块级实验及不同初始化条件下，SGF在主体身份保持、背景/布局一致性、时间稳定性方面均优于Self Forcing，仅用5秒训练窗口即可外推生成持续数分钟的视频。

结论：SGF通过恢复历史上下文梯度信号有效解决了自回归视频生成中的上下文-梯度差距问题，实现了更强的长视频外推能力，代码和模型将开源以推动该领域研究。

原始摘要：Recent autoregressive video diffusion methods are increasingly built upon Self Forcing, where the student is trained on histories produced by its own rollout rather than ground-truth video contexts. This reduces exposure bias, but the historical key-value cache is still used by future frames only as frozen rollout state. As a result, future losses cannot supervise how earlier generated latents should be written into more useful keys and values for later video-latent generation. We call this the historical context-gradient gap. We propose Self Gradient Forcing (SGF), a two-pass training strategy that restores this missing supervision signal without backpropagating through the full serial rollout. Pass 1 performs a no-gradient autoregressive rollout matching inference and, at a sampled denoising exit step, records both the self-generated context and the noisy latents fed to the model. Pass 2 performs parallel context-gradient reconstruction for the recorded exit step. The generated context is used as stop-gradient clean-latent input, while the model recomputes the context KV representations and future-to-context causal attention. Thus, SGF provides the missing memory-writing supervision within the native autoregressive training objective, using losses on future video latents to train the model to encode context into more effective causal memory. Across extensive long-horizon frame-wise and chunk-wise experiments under different initializations, SGF achieves stronger native long-video extrapolation than Self Forcing, especially in subject identity, background/layout consistency, and temporal stability. Remarkably, using only a 5-second training window, SGF can extrapolate to videos lasting several minutes. Code and models will be released to advance research on autoregressive video generation.

### [7] [Multi-Mask Diffusion Language Models for Few-Step Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19686)
*Sijin Chen,Yinuo Ren,Heyang Zhao,Ziheng Cheng,Quanquan Gu,Lexing Ying*

主分类：cs.CL

一句话总结：本文提出多掩码扩散模型(MultiMDM)，通过前向过程中将每个干净token先推向指定掩码再混合的机制，保留掩码结构以实现少步生成，并推导了闭式ELBO训练目标和离散状态一致性蒸馏方案。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有掩码扩散模型(MDM)因前向轨迹坍缩到单一全掩码状态，缺乏终端熵，难以实现一致性式少步生成；而基于均匀态扩散的替代方案虽避免退化，但难以区分干净token与噪声，损害建模质量和训练效率。

方法：提出MultiMDM：前向过程中每个干净token先推向指定掩码，再在掩码集上逐渐混合；反向过程通过预测指定掩码再细化为干净token实现草稿能力。推导了闭式ELBO训练目标，支持从预训练MDM连续训练；提出纯离散态一致性蒸馏方案，使用共享Gumbel耦合降低路径熵。

结果：在预训练和蒸馏实验上，MultiMDM为原则性少步生成提供了有效基础。

结论：MultiMDM通过保留掩码结构并引入多掩码机制，在少步生成场景下优于现有MDM和均匀态扩散方法。

原始摘要：Masked diffusion models (MDMs) are a promising family of language generators, but achieving high-quality few-step generation remains challenging. In MDMs, all forward trajectories collapse to a single fully masked state, leaving no terminal entropy for consistency-style few-step generation. While recent few-step alternatives based on uniform-state diffusion avoid this degeneracy, it becomes harder to distinguish clean tokens from noise than MDMs, which usually harms modeling quality and training efficiency. In this work, we propose a multi-mask diffusion model (MultiMDM) that preserves the masking structure towards few-step generation. In the forward process, each clean token is first pushed towards a designated mask and then gradually mixes over the mask set. As a result, the backward process has a drafting capability by predicting a designated mask before refining to a clean token. We derive a closed-form ELBO training objective for MultiMDM that supports continual training from pretrained MDMs. In addition, we formulate a purely discrete-state consistency distillation scheme, with a shared-Gumbel coupling to reduce pathwise entropy. Experiments on pretraining and distillation show that MultiMDM provides an effective foundation for principled few-step generation.

</details>
