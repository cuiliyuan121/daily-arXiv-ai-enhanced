### [1] [RGBX-Next: Towards Realistic Generative Rendering from G-Buffers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13929)
*Zheng Zeng,Marco Salvi,Lifan Wu,Jan Novák,Daqi Lin,Saeed Hadadan,Yichen Sheng,Robert Pottorff,Shiqiu Liu,Ravi Ramamoorthi,Ling-Qi Yan,Miloš Hašan*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出RGBX-Next，一个统一的前向和逆向生成渲染框架，利用扩散变换器（DiT）模型，既可以从图像/视频/流中估计G-buffer，也可以从G-buffer生成逼真的图像/视频/流，实现可控的生成式渲染。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：扩散模型在图像、视频和流生成上取得了显著成果，但与传统3D渲染相比，缺乏对生成输出的精确控制。作者认为可行路径是将生成模型作为以传统渲染的G-buffer为条件的可学习渲染器，从而实现可控生成。

方法：该方法的关键贡献是一个通用的微调配方，将扩散变换器（DiT）模型微调为生成式前向和逆向渲染器。其中前向渲染从G-buffer生成逼真图像/视频/流，逆向渲染从图像/视频/流估计G-buffer。

结果：实验结果表明，RGBX-Next在逼真生成渲染和内在分解（intrinsic decomposition）任务中均取得了高质量结果，验证了该框架的有效性。

结论：作者将公开发布所有模型，并认为该论文提出的设计原则将有益于未来可控生成式前向和逆向渲染的研究。

原始摘要：Diffusion models have achieved impressive results in image, video, and streaming generation. However, compared to traditional 3D rendering, they still lack precise control over the generated output. We believe a viable path forward is to use generative models as learned renderers conditioned on traditionally rendered G-buffers. We introduce RGBX-Next, a unified generative framework for forward and inverse rendering, which allows estimating G-buffers from images, videos, and streams, and rendering realistic images, videos, and streams from G-buffers. Our key contribution is a general recipe for finetuning diffusion transformer (DiT) models into generative forward and inverse renderers. We show that the resulting models achieve high quality in both realistic generative rendering and intrinsic decomposition. We will make all our models publicly available. We believe that the design principles presented in this paper will benefit future research on controllable generative forward and inverse rendering.

### [2] [ForgeWM: Progressive Causal Training for Few-Step Action-Conditioned Video World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14022)
*Xinye Li,Lingshuai Lin,Lei Wang,Liuzhou Zhang,Jialin Cui,Qingshan Li,Guanchu Wang,Qingbin Liu,Xi Chen,Jiang Bian,Wai Lam*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出ForgeWM框架，通过渐进式四阶段训练流程（域适应、教师强制的因果训练、因果一致性蒸馏、在线策略分布匹配），将双向动作条件视频生成器转化为高效少步交互式世界模型，支持1、2、4步稳态去噪预算，并支持双路径部署协议（延迟关键交互+可选的回放精炼），在Minecraft和FPS游戏轨迹上验证了其卓越的图像质量、动作对齐精度和控制准确率。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：动作条件视频世界模型需要低延迟因果生成和对游戏原生化控制输入的可靠响应。虽然因果蒸馏已实现单步或少步视频合成，但将其扩展到交互式世界模型仍具挑战性，因为离散键盘状态和连续鼠标运动必须在因果训练和自回归滚动过程中与时间压缩的潜在块保持对齐。

方法：提出ForgeWM渐进式框架，包含四个阶段：1）域适应（domain adaptation）；2）教师强制的因果训练（teacher-forced causal training）；3）因果一致性蒸馏（causal consistency distillation）；4）与双向教师进行在线策略分布匹配（on-policy distribution matching）。生成的预算专用学生模型在1、2、4步稳态去噪预算下运行。此外支持双路径部署协议，其中单步学生模型可重新加噪并精炼其保存的草稿。

结果：在配对的Minecraft轨迹上，ForgeWM在图像质量、参考对齐的运动轮廓一致性、动作信号准确率和鼠标控制准确率方面均领先评估系统，同时实现最低的参考LPIPS。该四阶段配方可迁移至手柄控制的FPS游戏玩法。回放精炼匹配四步参考质量，同时比从噪声重新生成更接近经验轨迹约三倍。

结论：ForgeWM证明了其在可控少步视频生成方面的有效性，通过渐进式训练策略成功解决了交互式世界模型中因果生成与动作控制的挑战，实现了高质量、低延迟、动作精准的交互式视频世界模型。

原始摘要：Action-conditioned video world models require low-latency causal generation and reliable responses to game-native controls. Although causal distillation enables one- or few-step video synthesis, extending it to interactive world models remains challenging, as discrete keyboard states and continuous mouse motion must remain aligned with temporally compressed latent chunks during causal training and autoregressive rollout. We introduce ForgeWM, a progressive framework that transforms a bidirectional action-conditioned video generator into efficient few-step world models through domain adaptation, teacher-forced causal training, causal consistency distillation, and on-policy distribution matching with a bidirectional teacher. The resulting budget-specialized students operate at steady-state denoising budgets of 1, 2, and 4 steps. ForgeWM further supports a dual-path deployment protocol combining latency-critical interaction with optional replay-time refinement, where the one-step student re-noises and refines its saved draft. On paired Minecraft trajectories, ForgeWM leads the evaluated systems in Imaging Quality, reference-aligned motion-profile agreement, action-sign accuracy, and mouse-control accuracy, while achieving the lowest reference LPIPS; the same four-stage recipe transfers to gamepad-controlled FPS gameplay. Replay-time refinement matches four-step reference quality while remaining roughly three times closer to the experienced trajectory than regeneration from noise. These results demonstrate ForgeWM's effectiveness for controllable few-step video generation.

</details>


### [3] [Beyond Text Conditioning: A Systematic Study of MLLM-DiT Fusion for Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14043)
*Yanbo Ding,Yijia Fan,Caihua Shan,Yifan Yang,Yifei Shen,Weijie Wang,Xirui Hu,Dongsheng Li,Lili Qiu,Yuqing Yang,Yali Wang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出BiVidGen，一个将多模态大语言模型（MLLM）与扩散Transformer（DiT）混合的视频生成框架。MLLM首先生成语义视觉token，DiT在文本和这些token的双重条件下通过多层交叉注意力渲染视频，显著提升语义对齐和时间连贯性。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：扩散Transformer已成为高保真视频生成的范式，但其高层语义规划能力有限。虽然MLLM与扩散模型混合架构在图像合成中表现出优势，但在视频生成中尚未充分探索，且现有方法通常将MLLM仅作为冻结特征编码器而非语义生成器使用。作者希望填补这一空白，系统研究MLLM应如何在视频生成中与DiT集成。

方法：作者通过回答三个关键问题系统研究集成方案：MLLM与DiT之间应使用何种中间表示（发现EMA-based tokenizer产生的离散语义视觉token最稳定且具表达力）、MLLM应如何生成该表示（发现自回归因果建模最有效）、DiT应如何在扩散渲染中融入该表示（发现显式视觉token条件化优于提示精炼或潜在桥接）。基于这些发现，BiVidGen中MLLM先自回归生成语义视觉token，DiT通过多层交叉注意力在文本和视觉token双重条件下渲染视频。

结果：大量实验表明，BiVidGen相比微调后的DiT基线在语义对齐和时间连贯性上有显著提升，在VBench-Long基准上取得更强的性能。

结论：显式的MLLM视觉规划为文生视频生成提供了超越纯文本条件化的有效中间接口，验证了将MLLM作为语义生成器而非冻结编码器的混合架构设计思路。

原始摘要：Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) have become the dominant paradigm for high-fidelity video generation, yet their ability to perform high-level semantic planning remains limited. While hybrid architectures integrating MLLMs with diffusion backbones have shown strong advantages in image synthesis, such designs remain underexplored in video generation, where existing approaches often treat MLLMs primarily as frozen feature encoders rather than semantic generators. To fill this gap, we systematically study how an MLLM should be integrated with a DiT for video generation by answering three questions: what intermediate representation should bridge the MLLM and DiT, how the MLLM should generate it, and how the DiT should incorporate it during diffusion rendering. Our analysis reveals three key findings: (1) discrete semantic visual tokens produced by an EMA-based tokenizer provide a stable and expressive interface, (2) autoregressive causal modeling is effective for generating these tokens, and (3) explicit visual-token conditioning is more effective than prompt refinement or latent bridging. Based on these findings, we propose BiVidGen, a hybrid framework where an MLLM first generates semantic visual tokens and a DiT renders videos conditioned on both text and these tokens via multi-layer cross-attention. Extensive experiments show that BiVidGen improves semantic alignment and temporal coherence over a fine-tuned DiT baseline, achieving stronger performance on VBench-Long. These results demonstrate that explicit MLLM-based visual planning provides an effective intermediate interface for text-to-video generation beyond text-only conditioning.

</details>


### [4] [Concept Guidance: Precise, Training-Free Latent Control for Text-to-Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14172)
*Nikolai Röhrich,Isabell Hans,Felix Krause,Björn Ommer*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：提出概念引导（CoG）方法，通过度量各层在特定概念上的互信息差异并加权组合去噪预测，实现对文本到图像扩散模型的概念级精确控制，无需额外训练。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：文本到图像扩散模型存在两大缺陷：(1)缺乏内在机制进行连续、概念特定的引导（如美感控制）；(2)对高局部一致性任务（如文本或手部生成）可靠性不足。

方法：提出概念互信息的概念，发现不同层对特定结构的生成存在局部分布差异。CoG方法量化每层概念特定影响，通过跳过概念相关层生成的预测进行加权组合来引导去噪过程。无需额外训练、外部模型、梯度或提示工程。

结果：在PixArt-alpha、SD3、SD3.5和FLUX.1-dev等流行模型上展示了对各种目标的性能提升。

结论：CoG是一种精确的目标特定引导方法，可直接用于现有模型，有效解决了概念级连续引导和局部一致性两大难题。

原始摘要：Text-to-image diffusion models have two major drawbacks that severely limit their practical utility: (1) standard models lack an intrinsic mechanism for continuous, concept-specific guidance (e.g., for precisely controlling how aesthetically pleasing an image looks), and (2) they lack reliability for tasks requiring high local coherence (e.g., generating text or human hands). To tackle these issues, we introduce a novel notion of concept-wise mutual information and find large, concept-dependent differences between individual layers, demonstrating that the generation of specific structures is localized in distinct parts of the network. We exploit this insight by reinforcing the impact of concept-relevant layers in Concept Guidance (CoG), a precise, target-specific guidance method that works for models out-of-the-box without additional training, external models, gradients, or prompt engineering. CoG first quantifies each layer's concept-specific impact and then guides denoising using a weighted combination of predictions generated with concept-relevant layers skipped. We demonstrate performance increases across various targets and popular models like PixArt-alpha, SD3, SD3.5, and FLUX.1-dev. Code is available at https://github.com/CompVis/concept_guidance

</details>


### [5] [Marionette: Predicting World States, Rendering Geometry, Painting Appearance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14530)
*Zian Meng,Zhen Li,Chuanhao Li,Qiang Li,Kaipeng Zhang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：提出了Marionette，一种将世界状态显式建模与固定零参数渲染器结合的游戏世界模型，将几何计算从神经生成中分离，实现长时程一致性、可控性和可修复性。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：传统交互式游戏世界模型直接在像素或潜空间自回归生成视觉观测，迫使姿态、几何、遮挡等结构化属性由同一生成序列隐式维护。长时程下潜世界属性误差累积，一致性和可控性脆弱。

方法：两阶段自回归动力学模型预测显式可解释的276维3D世界状态（多实体骨架、度量根轨迹、旋转）；零参数图形桥接将预测状态转为姿态控制视频，闭式计算空间几何与遮挡；控制条件视频扩散观测模型合成逼真RGB观测。

结果：预测世界状态直接可控：强制不匹配动作流使根对齐关节误差改变31%；长时程行为由状态决定且可在状态中修复——施加地形碰撞器和分离上限两条规则后穿透减少66%，角色保持互动；FVD为831，对比记录姿态的799，保真度无损。

结论：显式世界状态建模与零参数渲染器结合，可实现长时程可控制、可修复的世界模型，且不牺牲生成质量。

原始摘要：Interactive game world models typically autoregress visual observations directly in pixel or latent space, forcing structured properties such as pose, geometry, and occlusion to be implicitly maintained by the same generative sequence. Over long horizons, errors in these latent world properties accumulate, making consistency and controllability fragile. We explicitly model the evolving world state, delegate exact geometric computation to a fixed, zero-parameter renderer, and leave the neural model to synthesize appearance. We instantiate this idea as Marionette, a world model for interactive games with articulated characters. First, a two-stage autoregressive dynamics model predicts an explicit and interpretable 276-dimensional 3D world state comprising multi-entity articulated skeletons, metric root trajectories, and rotations. Second, a zero-parameter graphics bridge converts the predicted state into pose-control videos, computing world-space geometry and occlusion in closed form. Third, a control-conditioned video-diffusion observation model synthesizes photorealistic RGB observations from the resulting structured controls. Our experiments establish two properties of Marionette. First, the predicted world state is directly controllable. Forcing a mismatched action stream changes root-aligned joint error by 31% across 48 held-out segments. Second, long-horizon behaviour is determined in the state, and can be repaired there. Left free, the two generated characters drift to 21.2 m apart (recorded sessions stay near 5 m) and a third of frames show ground penetration. Two rules imposed on the explicit state, a terrain collider and a separation cap, cut penetration by 66% and keep the pair engaged, with no change to the observation model. Routing appearance through the predicted state costs no fidelity we can detect, at an FVD of 831 against 799 for recorded pose.

</details>
