### [1] [Understanding and Exploiting Diagonal Attention Sparsity in Autoregressive Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19702)
*Daeun Kim,Junwha Hong,Changhun Oh,Yoonsung Kim,Yoonhyeong Lee,Jongse Park*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文首次系统刻画了自回归图像生成中的注意力稀疏性，发现其独特性质（prefill-decode不对称、注意力集中于prompt与局部token、对角线稀疏模式），并据此提出对角线感知稀疏注意力机制，在GPU服务系统上实现最高3.1倍吞吐与1.19倍延迟改善，质量下降低于2%。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自回归图像生成与Transformer-based LLM服务设施兼容，但每次请求需生成数千个视觉token，导致解码阶段受KV cache访问瓶颈制约。视觉生成应用可容忍适度质量下降，因此稀疏注意力颇具吸引力。然而现有稀疏注意力主要针对文本LLM推理，其稀疏性假设能否适用于自回归图像生成尚不明确，故需系统研究。

方法：对多种工作负载和代表性开源模型进行首次系统性注意力稀疏性刻画，发现三大特性：明显的prefill-decode不对称、注意力强烈集中于prompt与局部token、源自视觉token空间局部性的独特对角线稀疏模式。基于此提出对角线感知稀疏注意力（diagonal-aware sparse attention），在最近窗口内沿对角线注意力方向选择性跳过KV条目。实现基于FlexGen、FlashAttention-2及自定义kernel的GPU服务系统。

结果：相比稠密推理，实现最高3.1倍吞吐提升和1.19倍延迟改善，质量下降低于2%。

结论：自回归图像生成的注意力稀疏性具有区别于文本LLM的独特性质，针对这些性质设计的对角线感知稀疏注意力可在保证质量的前提下显著提升服务效率。

原始摘要：Autoregressive image generation has emerged as a paradigm for multimodal AI systems due to its compatibility with transformer-based LLM serving infrastructures. However, generating thousands of visual tokens per request makes decoding increasingly bottlenecked by KV cache accesses during attention computation. Sparse attention is particularly attractive for this workload because many visual generation applications tolerate moderate quality degradation in exchange for improved performance and efficiency. While sparse attention has been extensively explored for text-based LLM inference, it remains unclear whether its sparsity assumptions generalize effectively to autoregressive image generation. We present the first systematic characterization of attention sparsity in autoregressive image generation across diverse workloads and representative open-source models. Our analysis reveals several distinguishing properties, including a pronounced prefill-decode asymmetry, strong attention concentration on prompt and local tokens, and a unique diagonal attention sparsity pattern arising from the spatial locality of visual tokens. Motivated by these observations, we propose a diagonal-aware sparse attention mechanism that selectively skips KV entries along the diagonal attention direction within a recent window. Implemented on top of a GPU-based serving system using FlexGen, FlashAttention-2, and custom kernels, our approach achieves up to 3.1x throughput and 1.19x latency improvements with less than 2% quality degradation compared to dense inference.

### [2] [Recency Forcing: Bridging the Long-Horizon Gap in Autoregressive Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19729)
*Tri Cao,Hung Nguyen,Phong Nguyen,Khoi Nguyen*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：该论文识别出自回归视频生成中的"KV Eviction Mismatch"训练-推理差异问题，提出Recency Forcing方法，通过对注意力logits施加时间步相关偏置，无需修改上下文长度或训练目标即可实现零额外开销的长时程视频生成，达到SOTA。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自回归视频生成在长时程上性能退化，原因是训练与推理之间存在的KV缓存驱逐不匹配（KV eviction mismatch）：训练时短片段的所有上下文帧都在KV缓存中，而推理时受内存限制，远处帧被驱逐，导致模型对上下文的依赖被破坏。传统的上下文截断模拟驱逐的方法会丢弃模型仍需要的时序信息，损害运动连贯性。

方法：1) 保留上下文但渐进性降低远处帧的影响，使最终驱逐影响可忽略；2) 引入位置响应R(Δ, t_denoise)作为基于扰动的敏感度度量，揭示上下文影响随时序距离急剧衰减且随去噪步系统变化；3) 提出Recency Forcing，基于R在pre-softmax注意力logits上施加非正、时间步相关偏置（TRB）；4) 提出Biased Attention Reparameterization (BAR)，将偏置移到softmax外部，使TRB变为标准FlashAttention调用，零开销；5) 支持免训练和训练两种模式。

结果：在VBench和VBench-Long上达到SOTA的长时程生成质量，且无额外推理成本。

结论：Recency Forcing通过闭合训练-推理差距，在不修改上下文长度或训练目标的前提下，有效解决了自回归视频生成的长时程退化问题，兼顾质量与效率。

原始摘要：Autoregressive (AR) video generation degrades over long horizons due to an overlooked train-inference discrepancy we term KV eviction mismatch: models train on short clips where all context frames reside in the KV cache, but at inference, memory constraints force distant frames to be evicted from the KV cache - removing context the model was conditioned on. Rather than simulating eviction via context truncation - which discards temporal information the model still needs and degrades motion coherence - we keep the context but while progressively reducing the influence of distant frames, making their eventual eviction negligible. To guide this design, we introduce the positional response $R( Δ, \, t_{\text{denoise}})$, a perturbation-based sensitivity measure revealing that context influence decays steeply with temporal distance and varies systematically across denoising steps. Motivated by this analysis, we propose Recency Forcing, which applies a non-positive, timestep-dependent bias, termed Temporal Response Bias (TRB), on pre-softmax attention logits derived directly from $R$, closing the train-inference gap without modifying context length or training objectives. We further introduce Biased Attention Reparameterization (BAR), an exact reformulation that moves the bias outside the softmax, making TRB a standard FlashAttention call at zero overhead. Recency Forcing operates in both training-free mode and training-based mode. Experiments on VBench and VBench-Long demonstrate state-of-the-art long-horizon generation quality at no additional inference cost.

</details>


### [3] [Astronex-World 1.0: Real-Time Interactive World Model Foundation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20034)
*Xin Zhou,Cong Miao*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：Astronex-World 1.0 是一个开源可控视频世界模型，支持文本/图像到视频生成，具备相机轨迹、连续动作和具身标识控制，5B 参数在消费级/中端 GPU 上实现实时流式生成并在 WBench 基准上超过部分更大模型。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：构建一个开源、可控的视频世界模型基础，使模型能在指定相机轨迹、连续动作和具身标识下预测未来视觉状态，并可在 rollout 指定位置插入文本事件，从而服务于世界建模、具身智能与自动驾驶等需要持久可控生成的应用。

方法：基于 Wan2.2-TI2V-5B 先验，提供双向全上下文生成模型与带块因果注意力、跨块 KV 缓存的因果模型。使用 PRoPE 注入相机内外参，用 64 维动作流调制每个 Transformer 层。训练分五阶段：双向相机与动作控制、骨干转块因果生成、少步学生蒸馏、恢复混合域动态、非对称 DMD/DMD2 分布匹配。

结果：因果模型可生成 832x480、24fps 视频；全部五阶段训练仅用两块 NVIDIA L20 48GB GPU，因果模型可在单卡实时流式生成。WBench Navi 得分 73.5，WBench Full 得分 70.0。在 Full 上，该 5B 模型超过 13.6B 的 LongCat-Video 和 14B 的 Helios，与 22B 的 LTX-2.3 相差不到 1 分，并超过在同一 5B 先验上于 NVIDIA A100 后训练的 YUME 1.5。

结论：该工作以较低参数量和较少的硬件资源实现了可控、实时的视频世界模型，性能具有竞争力。预留的动作输入输出接口支持面向具身智能与自动驾驶的后训练。

原始摘要：We present Astronex-World 1.0, an open controllable video world-model foundation. Given a text prompt (text-to-video) or an initial observation (image-to-video), the model predicts future visual states under frame-aligned camera trajectories, continuous actions, and an embodiment identifier, and accepts text events inserted at a specified position of a rollout. The family provides a bidirectional model for full-context generation and a causal model with block-causal attention and cross-block KV caching for persistent generation, both built on the Wan2.2-TI2V-5B prior. PRoPE injects camera intrinsics and extrinsics, while a 64-dimensional action stream modulates every Transformer layer. A five-stage training path develops bidirectional camera and action control, converts the backbone to block-causal generation, distills a few-step student, restores mixed-domain dynamics, and applies asymmetric DMD/DMD2 distribution matching. The causal model generates 832x480 video at 24 fps. All five training stages run on two NVIDIA L20 48 GB GPUs, and the causal model streams in real time on one. It scores 73.5 on WBench Navi and 70.0 on WBench Full. On Full, this 5B model is above the 13.6B LongCat-Video and the 14B Helios, within one point of the 22B LTX-2.3, and above YUME 1.5, which is post-trained from the same 5B prior on NVIDIA A100 GPUs. The reserved action input and output interfaces allow post-training for embodied intelligence and autonomous driving.

### [4] [CleanVideo: Adaptive Concept Erasure for Text-to-Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20267)
*Junchi Liao,Hongji Li,Wenrui Zhou,Lijie Hu*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出CleanVideo，一种面向视频扩散模型的选择性概念擦除框架，通过低维子空间干预与三模态门控机制实现"何处、何时、是否"干预，在保持视觉保真度与时间一致性的同时有效擦除目标概念。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：概念擦除已在图像生成模型中有所应用，但扩展到视频时面临困难：目标概念在视频中逐渐显现，且跨帧、跨去噪步变化，固定干预往往错失目标或引入模糊、抖动与内容失真，因此需要一种动态自适应的擦除方法。

方法：提出CleanVideo框架，采用低维子空间干预，并由三模态门控机制控制。该机制联合处理时空视觉特征、时间步信号与文本语义，以决定干预的位置、时机与是否干预；当替代概念可明确界定且非目标内容得以保留时，将待擦除内容引导至自然的替代概念。

结果：在三个视频扩散模型上的实验表明，CleanVideo能有效擦除目标概念，同时保持视觉保真度与时间一致性，在帧级与视频级评估中均优于现有基线；在受保护流水线保持完整的情况下，对概念恢复攻击也表现更优。

结论：CleanVideo通过动态、选择性的子空间干预，将概念擦除成功从图像扩展到视频领域，兼顾擦除效果与生成质量，并在对抗概念恢复攻击时具有更强鲁棒性。

原始摘要：Concept erasure aims to selectively eliminate undesired visual semantics from pre-trained generative models without compromising their general utility. Extending concept erasure from images to video is nontrivial. Target concepts emerge gradually and vary across frames and denoising steps. As a result, fixed interventions may miss the target or introduce blurring, jitter, and content distortion. We propose CleanVideo, a selective erasure framework that performs low-dimensional subspace intervention controlled by a tri-modal gating mechanism. By jointly processing spatiotemporal visual features, timestep signals, and textual semantics, CleanVideo determines where, when, and whether to intervene, steering erased content toward natural surrogate concepts when such surrogates can be clearly defined while preserving non-target content. Experiments on three video diffusion models show that CleanVideo effectively erases target concepts while maintaining visual fidelity and temporal coherence, outperforming existing baselines under frame-level and video-level evaluations and under concept-recovery attacks when the protected pipeline remains intact.

### [5] [Paint-Anything: Unified Any-Color Control for Image Generation and Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20816)
*Ji Xie,Dewei Zhou,Xinyu Huang,Zhennan Chen,Xun Wang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出 Paint-Anything，通过学习统一的十六进制颜色提示接口，实现任意精确色的图像生成与编辑。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：专业设计需要精确到 24 位十六进制值的任意颜色控制，而现有方法多依赖专用颜色表示或复杂推理流程；同时大语言模型已具备将十六进制值与颜色语义关联的能力，为简化方案提供了基础。

方法：构建数据管线，经目标定位、感知颜色标注与编辑对合成，从真实图像构造 Paint-500K；用纯色锚点补充监督（其像素与十六进制值完全一致），且仅在低噪声训练中使用自然图像、高噪声时间步使用纯色锚点；引入 ACBench 基准（含 ACBench-T2I 与 ACBench-Edit）衡量对象级十六进制颜色保真度。

结果：在 FLUX.2-4B 上，Paint-Anything 使 ACBench-T2I 与 ACBench-Edit 分数分别相对基模型提升 85.3% 与 28.3%；消融实验支持该训练配方，并在对比方法中取得最高的平均 CompColor 分数。

结论：以统一十六进制提示接口结合真实图像与纯色锚点监督，可有效实现对象级任意精确色的生成与编辑，并具备良好的评测与性能表现。

原始摘要：Professional design requires any-color control: the ability to specify an object's target color with any 24-bit hex value for image generation and editing. Prior work has explored color generation, editing, and colorization, but often relies on dedicated color representations or specialized inference procedures. Advances in large language models offer a simpler starting point: even compact models can associate hex values with color semantics. We present Paint-Anything, which learns a shared hex-prompt interface for generation and editing through object-level color supervision. We develop a data pipeline that constructs Paint-500K from real images through object grounding, perceptual color labeling, and editing-pair synthesis. Since shadows make real-image labels only approximate colors, we complement this supervision with pure-color anchors whose pixels exactly match their paired hex values. These anchors are used only at high-noise timesteps, leaving low-noise training to natural images. We further introduce Any Color Benchmark (ACBench), comprising ACBench-T2I and ACBench-Edit, to measure object-level hex color fidelity across both tasks. On FLUX.2-4B, Paint-Anything improves ACBench-T2I and ACBench-Edit scores by 85.3% and 28.3%, respectively, relative to the base model, with ablations supporting the training recipe. It also achieves the highest average CompColor score among the compared methods.

### [6] [Can 4D Foundation Models Remember?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20819)
*Guangzhao He,Hadar Averbuch-Elor,Wei-Chiu Ma*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：论文提出PersistBench基准（数据集与指标套件），用360°视频作为全局真值，从物体恒存、运动连续性、外观保持三方面评估4D基础模型的视觉记忆能力，发现现有模型仅能维持短期一致性，物体离开视野后性能显著下降。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：感知与记忆视觉世界是导航和交互的基础。现有4D基础模型（如相机可控视频模型、4D重建模型）能感知并重建动态环境，但它们"记得"所感知内容的能力尚不明确。已有基准多依赖像素级指标，且缺乏物体离开视野后的真值，无法以物体为中心、对照参考来评估视觉记忆。

方法：提出PersistBench，包含数据集与指标套件；以360°视频作为"全知"真值参考，设计三个评估维度：物体恒存（object permanence）、运动连续性（motion continuity）、外观保持（appearance preservation）；并对多类模型进行评估。

结果：跨多种类别的模型评估显示，当前模型只能维持短期一致性，一旦物体离开视野，一致性便显著退化。

结论：研究揭示了当前模型能力与鲁棒视觉记忆之间的差距（"看到并不等于记住"），为未来4D基础模型的发展提供方向；数据集与代码已公开。

原始摘要：Perceiving and remembering the visual world is fundamental to navigating and interacting with our environment. Current 4D foundation models, such as camera-controllable video models or 4D reconstruction models, can perceive and reconstruct dynamic environments, but how well they remember what they have perceived remains an open question. Existing benchmarks largely rely on pixel-level metrics and lack ground truth for objects once they leave the field of view, making them unable to evaluate visual memory in an object-centric manner against references. To fill this gap, we introduce PersistBench, a dataset and metric suite that leverages 360° videos as omniscient ground truth and proposes three evaluation aspects: object permanence, motion continuity, and appearance preservation. Evaluating various models across diverse categories reveals that current models can only maintain short-term consistency that degrades significantly once objects leave the field of view. Our findings highlight the gap between current model capabilities and robust visual memory ("seeing is not remembering"), providing guidance for future development of 4D foundation models. Dataset and code are available on the project page: https://guangzhaohe.com/persistbench.

</details>
