### [1] [Partition the Support, Reconstruct the Residual: Training-Free Sparse Attention for Video Generation and World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18484)
*Pardis Taghavi,Reza Langari,Gaurav Pandey*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出SparsePR，一种无需训练的块稀疏注意力加速方法，通过响应耦合分区（Response-Coupled Partitioning）与探针拟合残差重建（Probe-Fitted Residual Reconstruction）相结合，在视频Transformer上实现了1.48x-2.61x的端到端加速，同时保持生成质量在22.0-26.0%执行对密度下不下降。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：无训练块稀疏注意力可加速视频Transformer，但仅靠逐行注意力集中度无法直接生成可执行的稀疏算子。查询共享块路由时支持集可能重叠不良，而保留的注意力质量本身不能决定跳过交互带来的softmax后误差，因此需要新的方法来同时考虑分区几何与残差预测。

方法：SparsePR包含两个核心组件：(1) 响应耦合分区（Response-Coupled Partitioning）：对采样查询的键响应形成配对的K/V组，利用质心诱导查询-响应对齐坐标以实现共享路由；(2) 探针拟合残差重建（Probe-Fitted Residual Reconstruction）：用少量精确查询行校准与调用相关的仿射校正，在探针残差观测的输出子空间内从稀疏输出重建残差。

结果：在四个异构视频生成与世界模型上，SparsePR始终降低注意力重建误差。消融实验表明探针拟合贡献了大部分误差降低，而响应耦合分区降低了硬丢弃误差并改善了有限探针预算下的重建。在22.0-26.0%实际执行对密度下保持生成质量，实现1.48x-2.61x端到端加速。

结论：分区几何同时影响合并支持性与剩余残差从稀疏输出的可预测性，SparsePR通过结合响应耦合分区和探针拟合残差重建有效解决了无训练块稀疏注意力中支持重叠差与误差不可预测的问题，在保证生成质量的同时显著加速视频Transformer推理。

原始摘要：Training-free block-sparse attention can accelerate video transformers, but row-wise attention concentration does not by itself specify an executable sparse operator. Queries sharing a block route may have poorly overlapping supports, while retained attention mass alone does not determine the post-softmax error from skipped interactions. We show that partition geometry affects both pooled support and the predictability of the remaining residual from the sparse output. We introduce SparsePR, which combines Response-Coupled Partitioning with Probe-Fitted Residual Reconstruction. Sampled-query key responses form paired K/V groups, whose centroids induce query-response coordinates for shared routing. A small set of exact query rows then calibrates a call-specific affine correction from the sparse output within the output subspace observed in the probe residuals. Across four heterogeneous video generation and world models, SparsePR consistently reduces attention-reconstruction error. Ablations show that probe fitting accounts for most of this reduction, while response-coupled partitioning lowers hard-drop error and improves reconstruction under a finite probe budget. SparsePR preserves generation quality at 22.0-26.0% realized executed-pair density while achieving 1.48x-2.61x end-to-end speedups. Project page: https://pardistaghavi.github.io/SparsePR-website/

### [2] [VA-Judger: Reward Modeling from Human Preference Feedback for Joint Video-Audio Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18607)
*Yinming Huang,Shuyuan Tu,Xi Yan,Zihan Yang,Jianhua Han,Xu Hang,Yu-Gang Jiang,Zuxuan Wu*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出VA-Judger，一个用于联合视频-音频生成的思维链全维度奖励模型，并构建了VAPref-10K人类偏好数据集和VA-Judger-Bench基准。该模型通过分阶段训练（质量差距学习、困难样本偏好蒸馏、维度级强化学习）来更好地对齐人类偏好，替代传统分别评估各质量维度的指标，从而减少奖励黑客问题并提升生成质量。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有的强化学习奖励信号由音频质量、视觉保真度和同步性等单独质量指标组合而成，这些指标分别评估感知维度，无法捕捉文本提示、视频和音频之间的整体语义和时间连贯性，容易导致奖励黑客行为——生成内容在指标上得分高但人类观感不连贯。

方法：1) 构建VAPref-10K大规模人类偏好数据集（9K提示词、10.3K细粒度成对比较）；2) 提出VA-Judger-Bench基准（含域内和域外模型比较）评估奖励模型与人类偏好的一致性；3) 提出VA-Judger思维链全维度奖励模型：先从质量差距明显的样本对学习结构化输出和粗粒度偏好判别，再通过拒绝采样与人类标注验证为接近质量的困难比较蒸馏可靠偏好解释，最后进行维度级强化学习，将人类反馈分解为各质量维度以提供比单一二值偏好标签更密集的奖励信号。

结果：实验表明VA-Judger在域内和域外评估中均优于指标基线在预测人类偏好上的表现。使用其人类对齐的奖励对音频-视频生成模型进行后训练，在生成质量上取得了显著提升。

结论：基于思维链和多阶段训练的全维度奖励模型能够更准确地捕捉人类对联合视频-音频生成的偏好，有效缓解奖励黑客问题，并显著改善下游生成模型的输出质量。

原始摘要：Using reinforcement learning to post-train joint video-audio generation models requires a reward signal. Existing methods construct this reward by combining metrics for individual quality dimensions, including audio quality, visual fidelity, and synchronization. However, these metrics evaluate perceptual dimensions separately and fail to capture the overall semantic and temporal coherence among the text prompt, video, and audio that shapes human preferences. Optimizing models against these metrics encourages reward hacking, generating video-audio content that achieves high scores on these metrics yet appears incoherent or unfaithful to human viewers. To address this problem, we first construct a large-scale human-preference dataset VAPref-10K for joint video-audio generation, comprising 9K prompts and 10.3K fine-grained paired comparisons from open-source generation models. We also introduce the VA-Judger-Bench benchmark with both in-domain and out-of-domain model comparisons to evaluate whether reward models truly align with human preferences. We further propose VA-Judger, a chain-of-thought omni-reward model for joint video-audio generation. In particular, VA-Judger first learns from pairs with clear quality gaps to establish structured output and coarse preference discrimination, then distills reliable preference explanations for harder near-quality comparisons via rejection sampling verified against human annotations, and finally performs dimension-wise reinforcement learning that decomposes human feedback into individual quality dimensions for denser reward signals than a single binary preference label. Experiments show that VA-Judger outperforms metric baselines in predicting human preferences on both in-domain and out-of-domain evaluations. Using its human-aligned rewards for post-training audio-video generation model also yields significant improvements in generation quality.

### [3] [Learning Canonical Register Automata over Ordered Data Domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18765)
*Yong Li,Qiyi Tang,Di-De Yen*

主分类：cs.CL

一句话总结：本文研究了有序数据域上确定性寄存器自动机（DRAs）的主动学习问题，提出了一个统一框架，覆盖稠密域（如有理数）和非稠密域（如整数），并实现了基于成员查询、等价查询和记忆性查询的多项式时间主动学习算法。该框架还推导出整数域上DRA最小化问题的可判定性，并改进了若干相关决策问题的复杂度上界。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：寄存器自动机是配备内存的有限自动机，可识别无限字母表上的数据语言。此前主动学习算法仅针对具有恒等测试的域，而对有序数据域（如稠密的有理数和非稠密的整数）上的学习问题缺乏统一处理。作者希望通过统一框架解决这一空白，并进一步推动最小化和决策问题的研究。

方法：作者提出了一个统一的主动学习框架，将稠密域和非稠密有序数据域上的DRA学习问题归约到同一算法框架中。该算法使用成员查询、等价查询和记忆性查询三种oracle，在多项式时间内完成学习。记忆性查询最初是为了学习具有恒等测试的DRA而引入的，本文将其扩展到有序域场景。

结果：实现了有序数据域上DRA的多项式时间主动学习算法；证明了非稠密有序域（整数）上DRA最小化问题可判定，扩展了此前仅对稠密域成立的结果；改进了有序域上DRA多个与学习查询相关的决策问题的复杂度上界。

结论：本文表明，通过统一的框架，稠密和非稠密有序数据域上的DRA主动学习问题可以被一致地解决，且该框架还带来了最小化可判定性和复杂度改进等额外理论成果。

原始摘要：Register automata are finite automata equipped with memory that recognize data languages over infinite alphabets. In this work, we investigate active learning algorithms for deterministic register automata (DRAs) over ordered data domains--covering both dense domains, such as the rationals, and non-dense domains such as the integers. We show that the active learning problem for DRAs over both dense and non-dense ordered domains can be treated within a single unified framework. More specifically, we develop and implement a polynomial-time active learning procedure for DRAs over ordered domains, using oracles for membership, equivalence and memorability queries. The memorability queries were originally introduced for learning DRAs over domains with identity tests. Our unified framework also leads to a new consequence: minimization of DRAs over the non-dense ordered domain of integers is decidable, extending a result previously known only for dense domains. Finally, we give improved complexity bounds of several decision problems for DRAs over ordered domains that are closely related to the queries used in active learning.

</details>
