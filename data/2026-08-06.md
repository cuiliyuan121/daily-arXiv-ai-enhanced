### [1] [OmniVR: Joint Video-Audio Conditional Generation for Restoring Degraded Historical Films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04224)
*Xin Lu,Zihao Fan,Mingchen Zhong,Jie Huang,Xueyang Fu,Zheng-Jun Zha*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：OmniVR是首个联合音视频生成式复原模型，基于22B参数的多模态DiT骨干，将复原形式化为条件生成任务，通过联合降质管线、T2AV到AV2AV的架构转换以及首帧I2V锚定等关键设计，同时处理历史影片中的视觉和音频退化问题，并在提出的OmniVRBench基准上超越所有现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：历史影片常同时遭受视觉和音频退化（模糊、噪声、闪烁、嘶嘶声、削波和丢失），现有方法独立修复各模态，导致质量缺口和跨模态不一致问题。

方法：提出三个关键设计：(1)联合音视频降质管线，从互联网收集数据模拟真实老片特征；(2)保持架构的T2AV到AV2AV转换，采用提示退火以最大程度保留生成先验；(3)首帧图像到视频(I2V)锚定，配合损失重加权和波形监督，用于长视频外推和音频保真度。

结果：OmniVR在所有六个视觉指标上超越所有先前方法，取得最佳音频质量，并产生自然的着色效果，是首个同时解决三个方面的联合修复方法。

结论：OmniVR证明联合音视频生成式复原比独立模态修复更有效，通过统一的DiT框架能够同时提升视觉质量、音频质量、时间一致性和音视频同步性，代码和权重将公开。

原始摘要：Historical films suffer from co-occurring visual and audio degradations---blur, noise, flicker, hiss, clipping, and dropout---yet existing methods restore each modality independently, leaving quality gaps and cross-modal inconsistency. We present OmniVR, the first joint audio-video generative restoration model. Built upon a 22B-parameter audio-video generation backbone, OmniVR formulates restoration as conditional generation within a unified multimodal DiT: the low-quality video and audio are encoded as latent conditions, combined with a fixed restoration prompt, and jointly denoised to recover visual structure, temporal motion, and acoustic detail under one coordinated objective. Three key designs enable this adaptation: (1) a joint audio-video degradation pipeline that simulates real old-film characteristics from Internet-collected data; (2) an architecture-preserving text-to-audio-video (T2AV) to audio-video-to-audio-video (AV2AV) transition with prompt annealing that maximally retains the generative prior; and (3) first-frame image-to-video (I2V) anchoring with loss reweighting and waveform supervision for long-video extrapolation and audio fidelity. We also propose OmniVRBench, the first benchmark that evaluates audio-video restoration across visual quality, audio quality, temporal consistency, and audio-visual synchrony on 200 real historical clips. OmniVR surpasses all prior methods on all six visual metrics, achieves the best audio quality, and produces natural colorization---the first method to jointly address all three aspects. Code and weights will be publicly released. Project Page: https://xin1u.github.io/OminiVR_PAGE/

### [2] [Poly-OPD: Heterogeneous Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Capability-Selectable Flow Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04349)
*Siming Fu,Haojun Xu,Ruizhe He,Zheming Fu,Hualiang Wang,Jie Huang,Xiaoxiao Ma,Mingchen Zhong,Weihu Huang,Xiaoxuan He,Linjiang Huang,Si Liu*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：Poly-OPD是一个框架，通过像素桥梁和DINOv2空间监督，将多个异构文生图教师模型的互补优势（如美学偏好和构图指令遵循）蒸馏到一个紧凑的流匹配学生模型中，最终在GenEval和DrawBench指标上超越所有教师模型。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有的开源文生图模型各有互补优势，但由于不同模型使用不同的自编码器和噪声调度，难以将这些优势直接迁移或合并到单一模型中。

方法：1) 通过像素桥梁进行on-policy蒸馏，弥合不同教师的不兼容潜在空间；2) 在冻结的DINOv2空间中进行监督，实现跨不兼容潜在空间的训练；3) 使用梯度兼容性诊断组织适配器——注意力LoRA共享、前馈适配器教师特定；4) 采用gap-aware课程学习，优先训练学生仍落后于教师的类别。

结果：将FLUX.1-dev和Z-Image蒸馏到2.5B的SD3.5-Medium学生模型中，GenEval从67.3提升到73.3（超过两个更大的教师），DrawBench HPSv3从9.34提升到11.35。

结论：Poly-OPD成功证明了将异构教师模型的互补能力整合到单一紧凑可切换模型中的可行性，在保持模型规模优势的同时实现了超越教师模型的综合性能。

原始摘要：Leading open text-to-image models often carry complementary strengths: one may lead on preference-aligned aesthetics while another follows compositional instructions more faithfully. However, differences in their autoencoders and noise schedules make it difficult to transfer these strengths across models. In this paper, we present Poly-OPD, a framework that can consolidate complementary strengths of heterogeneous teachers into a single compact flow-matching student. To bridge the incompatible latent spaces of different teachers, Poly-OPD performs on-policy distillation through a pixel bridge. Each student-generated image is re-encoded by a selected teacher's encoder and refined from a noise level matched by magnitude under the teacher's noise schedule. The resulting target is further matched to the student in frozen DINOv2 space, enabling supervision across incompatible latent spaces. To retain complementary capabilities without cross-teacher interference, Poly-OPD uses a gradient compatibility diagnostic to organize its adapters: attention LoRA modules are shared across teachers, whereas feed-forward adapters remain teacher-specific. During distillation, a gap-aware curriculum devotes more training to compositional categories where the student still falls short of the teacher. As each gap narrows, training shifts toward categories with larger remaining gaps. By distilling FLUX.1-dev and Z-Image into a 2.5B SD3.5-Medium student, Poly-OPD improves GenEval from 67.3 to 73.3, surpassing both larger teachers, and raises DrawBench HPSv3 from 9.34 to 11.35, consolidating both strengths within a switchable model.

</details>


### [3] [UniWorld-View: Large-Baseline View Synthesis via Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04701)
*Haiyang Zhou,Wangbo Yu,Chaoran Feng,Xunyu Zhou,Yonghong Tian,Li Yuan*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出UniWorld-View，一个统一框架，将显式3D几何引导与生成式扩散建模相结合，实现从单目输入进行可控的大基线新视角合成。该方法通过遮挡感知的点云渲染策略提供几何先验，结合视频扩散骨干网络，在极端相机运动下仍能生成高保真且几何一致的新视角，并支持下游动态3DGS重建。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：社交媒体上大量随意拍摄的单目视频和图像为沉浸式内容创作提供了宝贵来源，但从稀疏观测生成新视角充满挑战。现有方法中，基于重建的方法（如NeRF和3DGS）在稀疏输入下性能退化且无法显式处理遮挡；生成式方法虽降低了数据需求，但因几何引导不准确或隐性化，仍难以应对大基线视角合成。因此需要一种既能精确控制相机又能保证几何一致性的统一框架。

方法：UniWorld-View整合了显式3D引导与生成式扩散建模。具体包括：(1) 提出遮挡感知的点云渲染策略，解决可见性歧义并提供准确的几何先验；(2) 将该渲染策略与强大的视频扩散骨干网络耦合，利用扩散模型生成高保真新视角；(3) 框架支持精确相机控制和几何一致的大基线视角生成，并可进一步输出多视角视频用于下游动态3DGS重建。

结果：在WorldScore基准和零样本新视角合成基准上的实验表明，UniWorld-View在可控性、几何一致性和视觉保真度方面均表现出有效性，能够在极端相机运动和宽基线变化下生成高质量新视角。

结论：UniWorld-View通过结合遮挡感知的显式几何引导与生成式扩散建模，成功解决了大基线单目新视角合成中几何不一致和遮挡处理困难的问题，为沉浸式内容创作提供了有效的统一解决方案，并可作为动态3DGS重建的上游工具。

原始摘要：The abundance of casually captured monocular videos and images on social media provides a valuable source for immersive content creation, where generating novel views from such sparse observations can greatly enhance user experiences. However, producing photorealistic and geometrically consistent views with precise camera control remains challenging when input coverage is extremely limited. Reconstruction-based approaches such as NeRF and 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) deteriorate severely under sparse inputs and fail to explicitly handle occlusions. Generative methods ease data requirements but still struggle with large-baseline view synthesis due to inaccurate or implicit geometric guidance. To overcome these limitations, we introduce UniWorld-View, a unified framework for controllable large-baseline novel view synthesis from monocular inputs. UniWorld-View integrates explicit 3D guidance with generative diffusion modeling to enable precise camera control and geometrically consistent view generation. The geometric guidance is obtained through an occlusion-aware point cloud rendering strategy that resolves visibility ambiguities and provides accurate priors for diffusion-based synthesis. By coupling this rendering strategy with powerful video diffusion backbones, UniWorld-View achieves high-fidelity novel view generation even under extreme camera motions and wide-baseline changes, and can further provide multi-view videos for downstream dynamic 3DGS reconstruction. Experiments on the WorldScore benchmark and zero-shot NVS benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of UniWorld-View in controllability, geometric consistency, and visual fidelity.

### [4] [Rethinking Pixel Mean Flows via Interval Denoiser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04818)
*Alexander Zaytsev,Dmitry Baranchuk,Alexander Korotin,Aibek Alanov*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出Interval Denoiser，一个理论上严谨的无隐空间（latent-free）生成框架，直接从flow matching ODE推导出中间轨迹状态的精确解析映射，使得网络可直接在像素空间上进行回归训练。通过残余剪裁和时间采样课程，在ImageNet 256x256上一步生成FID达4.55，两步达3.98。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现代扩散和流模型趋向于少步数、无隐空间的生成方式，以避免多步采样的计算开销和外部自编码器的重建瓶颈。然而，直接对中间轨迹状态进行回归在像素空间上存在低维流形学习和梯度偏差等问题，缺乏理论严谨的解决方案。

方法：从flow matching ODE出发，推导出中间轨迹状态的精确解析映射（Interval Denoiser）。关键创新包括：(1) 证明预测位于低维流形上，使像素空间的网络回归可行；(2) 避免经验的代数替换，正确分离纯时间导数，防止梯度评估偏差并确保精确的一阶优化；(3) 引入残余剪裁（residual clipping）和时间采样课程（time-sampling curriculum），实现有效的长间隔训练。

结果：从零开始在ImageNet 256x256上训练，模型在一步（1-NFE）时达到FID 4.55，两步（2-NFE）时达到FID 3.98，且无需使用感知损失。

结论：Interval Denoiser提供了一种理论上严谨且实用的无隐空间少步生成方案，通过精确解析映射和针对性训练策略，在像素空间中实现了优异的少步生成性能，同时避免了外部自编码器的瓶颈和高昂的多步采样成本。

原始摘要：Modern diffusion and flow-based models are increasingly moving toward few-step, latent-free generation to bypass the computational overhead of multi-step sampling and the reconstruction bottlenecks of external autoencoders. We propose the Interval Denoiser, a theoretically rigorous framework for latent-free generation. Derived directly from the flow matching ODE, it establishes an exact analytical mapping for intermediate trajectory states. Unlike prior formulations, our prediction is shown to reside on a low-dimensional manifold across any time interval, making the regression tractable for a network operating directly on pixels. Furthermore, by avoiding empirical algebraic substitutions, our formulation correctly isolates the pure time derivative to prevent biased gradient evaluations and ensure exact first-order optimization. By analyzing this objective, we equip our framework with residual clipping and a time-sampling curriculum, enabling effective long-interval training and improving few-step performance. Trained from scratch on ImageNet 256x256, our model achieves an FID of 4.55 in one step (1-NFE) and 3.98 in two steps (2-NFE) without perceptual losses.

</details>


### [5] [ContextMaster: Interactive Multi-Shot Video Creation via Fixed-Budget Sparse Context Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04956)
*Xu Guo,Zhengxuan Wei,Xinghui Li,Hanzhuo Huang,Xinyu Liu,Xiangyang Luo,Min Wei,Yiran Zhu,Qiulin Wang,Yulong Xu,Xintao Wang,Pengfei Wan,Qi Fan,Xiangwang Hou*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出ContextMaster，一个统一的多镜头视频生成模型，通过角色感知上下文表示、固定预算稀疏上下文路由和两阶段特权上下文蒸馏框架，支持文本生成、参考约束和编辑等多种操作，在不增加推理成本的情况下保持多镜头间的一致性。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有视频模型虽然支持生成、参考条件化和编辑，但将这些操作视为独立任务，无法在多镜头视频创作中共享历史信息。实际创作需要跨多个镜头保持一致性，因此需要一个统一的交互式多镜头视频生成模型。

方法：提出了两阶段特权上下文蒸馏框架：第一阶段通过一致性蒸馏将密集教师模型的完整上下文行为迁移到学生模型，第二阶段通过分布匹配优化部署时的推演。同时设计了角色感知上下文表示、可复用的清理上下文状态、固定预算稀疏上下文路由以及ConstraintSink机制来保持任务约束可见性。

结果：在文本生成、参考约束和编辑三种基础任务上优于专门的基线模型，用户研究验证了灵活组合工作流的有效性，单GPU上达到16FPS的推理速度。

结论：ContextMaster通过统一模型与高效上下文管理机制，解决了交互式多镜头视频创作中历史保留与推理成本之间的权衡问题，为视频生成模型的实用化部署提供了有效方案。

原始摘要：Recent video models increasingly support generation, reference conditioning, and editing within a single model, yet typically expose them as separate operations over fixed inputs. Practical creation unfolds across multiple shots, requiring one model to generate from text, follow a reference, or edit source footage while maintaining shared history. We formalize this setting as interactive multi-shot video creation (IMVC) and introduce ContextMaster, a unified model with a role-aware context representation for these operations. An interactive model must retain access to an expanding history without allowing the context read cost at each denoising step to grow. ContextMaster combines reusable clean context states with fixed budget sparse context routing and uses ConstraintSink to keep task constraints visible. To address the dual challenges of sparse context access and inference with few denoising steps, we propose a two-stage privileged context distillation framework, which transfers full context behavior from a dense teacher through consistency distillation and then refines deployment rollouts with distribution matching. Experiments on the three primitive tasks demonstrate improved task fulfillment and consistency across shots over specialized baselines. User studies further validate flexibly composed workflows, while the model reaches 16 FPS on a single GPU.

### [6] [HelloWorld: Enabling Socially Interactive Characters in Video World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05070)
*Liangyang Ouyang,Ruicong Liu,Xuangeng Chu,Kaipeng Zhang,Yoichi Sato*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：HelloWorld是一个支持用户与视频世界模型中角色进行社交互动的视频世界模型。用户只需按下一个按钮，即可让屏幕中的角色朝向摄像头做出回应（如转身、挥手、点头或简短问候）。通过自蒸馏流程微调视频生成模型，并引入免训练模块控制交互发生的时间窗口，同时构建了包含400个样本的HelloWorldBench基准进行评测。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：尽管视频世界模型近期取得了显著进展，但用户与视频世界中角色之间的社交互动仍未被支持，这是现有模型的重要空白。

方法：提出自蒸馏流程，让视频生成模型在自身合成的数据上微调；每个合成片段同时包含社交交互和相机运动，使模型学会相机姿态条件而不降低交互质量。推理时引入免训练模块，通过调制DiT的交叉注意力掩码，使交互相关的文本提示仅作用于按下按钮的时间窗口内，实现角色回复的时间局部化。此外构建了HelloWorldBench基准（400样本，3个社交交互指标+3个常规指标）。

结果：实验表明HelloWorld在交互质量上超越了多种基线方法，同时保持了最先进的画面美学和相机姿态跟随能力。

结论：HelloWorld成功填补了视频世界模型缺乏社交交互能力的空白，通过自蒸馏和注意力掩码调制实现了自然且时间可控的角色互动，并在交互质量与生成质量上均达到领先水平。

原始摘要：Despite the remarkable recent progress of video world models, social interaction between users and the characters within these worlds remains unsupported. To fill this gap, we present HelloWorld, a video world model that enables social interaction with in-world characters. With a single button press, users can prompt the on-screen character to respond toward the camera, e.g., turning to the viewer, waving, nodding, or speaking a short greeting. To make these interactions natural, we propose a self-distillation pipeline that finetunes the video generation model on data synthesized by itself. Each synthesized clip contains both social interactions and camera motion, allowing the model to learn camera-pose conditioning without degrading interaction quality. At inference, we further introduce a training-free module that determines when the interaction occurs. Upon a button press, it modulates the cross-attention masks of the DiT so that the interaction-related text prompt attends only to the frames within the press window, temporally localizing the character's response. We further build HelloWorldBench, a 400-sample benchmark with three social interaction metrics alongside three conventional metrics, for evaluation. Experiments demonstrate that HelloWorld surpasses a variety of baselines in interaction quality, while maintaining state-of-the-art picture aesthetics and camera-pose following. Project page: https://github.com/AlayaLab/HelloWorld

</details>
