### [1] [MAD-HOI: Masked Autoregressive Diffusion for Generating Articulated Hand Object Interactions from Text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10162)
*Ananya Bal,Kartik Sharma,Ethan Lai,Samyak Tiwari,Liza Dahiya,Chaitanya Chawla,Laszlo A. Jeni*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出MAD-HOI模型，通过掩码自回归与扩散相结合的方式，实现文本驱动的手物交互（HOI）序列生成，支持变长生成、复合动作序列、动作补全与填充以及可靠的终止预测，在保持物理合理性的同时提升了生成多样性。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有文本到HOI序列生成方法存在局限：扩散模型只能生成原子动作且需预先指定长度，自回归方法依赖离散动作编码导致接触敏感细节丢失。需要一种兼具序列级灵活性（如变长、复合、补全）与连续细节保留的方法。

方法：MAD-HOI首先在手和物体运动的连续潜在空间中进行编码，保持流间解耦以维持逐流控制；随后通过掩码自回归Transformer预测上下文特征，用于条件化流匹配头（flow-matching head）。整个模型通过单一训练目标实现原子与复合动作生成、条件运动补全与填充以及EOM（动作结束）预测。

结果：在ARCTIC和GRAB数据集上进行了全面评估，实验表明相比其他开源基线方法，MAD-HOI生成的交互动作更加多样且物理上更合理。

结论：MAD-HOI通过融合掩码自回归与扩散/流匹配的优势，成功解决了HOI生成中序列灵活性不足和细节丢失的问题，为文本驱动的复杂人-物交互生成提供了统一且实用的解决方案。

原始摘要：Methods for text-based generation of hand-object interaction (HOI) sequences primarily focus on producing smooth, physically plausible trajectories. A truly utilitarian method should additionally support variable-length generation, composite motion sequences, motion completion and infilling, and reliable termination without compromising physical plausibility. Standard diffusion models for HOI generation are typically trained only for text-to-motion generation on atomic motions and require the motion length to be specified a-priori. Autoregressive (AR) methods provide greater sequence-level flexibility, but commonly depend on discrete motion codes, which can lose contact-sensitive motion detail. To address these key limitations, we present a model performing Masked Autoregression with Diffusion for HOI generation (MAD-HOI). Our method starts by encoding hand and object motions in a continuous latent space while keeping them disentangled to maintain stream-wise control. This is followed by a masked autoregressive transformer to predict context features that condition a flow-matching head. MAD-HOI is capable of motion generation for atomic and composite articulated sequences, conditioned motion completion and infilling, as well as EOM (End of Motion) prediction from a single training objective. We provide comprehensive evaluations for these capabilities and benchmark our method on the ARCTIC and GRAB datasets. Our experiments demonstrate that our method generates more diverse and physically plausible interactions compared to other open-sourced baseline methods.

### [2] [Stream Forcing: Constructing Unified Training Trajectory for Robust Streaming Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10439)
*Yueting Zhu,Yuehao Song,Kaicheng Zhang,Bao Tang,Shaoyu Chen,Qian Zhang,Wenyu Liu,Xinggang Wang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出Stream Forcing，一个统一的流式视频生成训练框架。通过将视频扩散采样重构为帧索引的随机过程（对噪声水平进行索引），构建从独立采样逐步演化为推理一致采样的连续训练轨迹，并引入联合校准算法和时间相关性采样算法来保证轨迹平滑性和跨帧相关性。在UCF-101基准上实现了36.6%的FVD改进，并支持零样本外推到长时程视频生成（27.9% FVD改进）。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：流式视频扩散模型存在训练-推理不匹配的根本问题：推理时遵循特定的去噪顺序，而先进的训练策略通常需要多样化的噪声水平配置。需要在训练-推理一致性和训练覆盖度之间做出权衡。

方法：将视频扩散采样重构为帧索引的随机过程；在该随机过程空间中构建连续训练轨迹，使采样调度从独立采样逐步演化为推理一致采样；提出联合校准算法和时序相关采样算法分别确保轨迹平滑性和跨帧相关性。

结果：在UCF-101基准上实现36.6%的FVD改进；在长时程视频生成的零样本外推任务上实现27.9%的FVD改进。

结论：Stream Forcing作为一个统一的训练框架，有效平衡了训练充分性和推理效率，显著提升了流式视频生成的质量，并展现了良好的长视频外推能力。

原始摘要：Streaming video generation holds strong potential for world modeling, where future frames must be inferred online sequentially to form a continuous video stream. However, streaming video diffusion models introduce a fundamental train-inference mismatch: inference follows a specialized denoising order, whereas advanced training strategies typically require diverse noise-level configurations. To address this trade-off between train-inference consistency and training coverage, we reformulate the video diffusion sampling as a frame-indexed stochastic process over noise levels. Within this stochastic process space, we construct a continuous training trajectory along which the sampling schedule progressively evolves from independent sampling to inference-consistent sampling. We further introduce a joint calibration algorithm and a temporal correlative sampling algorithm to ensure trajectory smoothness and cross-frame correlation. Building on these designs, we propose Stream Forcing, a unified training framework for streaming video generation that balances training sufficiency and inference efficiency. Extensive experiments demonstrate that Stream Forcing significantly improves generation quality with a 36.6% FVD improvement on the UCF-101 benchmark. Furthermore, our method facilitates robust zero-shot extrapolation to long-horizon video generation with a 27.9% FVD improvement on the UCF-101 benchmark.

### [3] [SparSTAR: Sparse Attention for SpaceTime AutoRegressive Video Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10519)
*Jongbeom Lee,Hyunwoo Yu,Jincheol Yang,Jaemin Choi,Suk-Ju Kang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出SparSTAR，一种针对视频视觉自回归生成模型的训练无关块稀疏注意力方法，通过在当前查询和键激活上为每个昂贵尺度评分连续键块，在保持所需条件上下文的同时执行前向稀疏路径，实现约1.6倍端到端加速，同时保持与稠密InfinityStar相近的生成质量。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：InfinityStar将视觉自回归生成扩展到视频，通过图像和片段金字塔序列实现，但其尺度变化和跨片段上下文导致后期尺度注意力计算成本高昂，且从扩散模型或图像VAR模型复用的稀疏模式不可靠，需要针对该场景设计专门的稀疏注意力方法。

方法：SparSTAR在每个昂贵的尺度和注意力头上，从当前查询和键激活中对连续键块进行评分，保留所需的条件上下文，并通过仅前向的稀疏路径执行选中的块。该方法基于三个分析维度：片段内跨尺度一致性、跨片段边界的模式持续性、以及跨远距离尺度复用的质量退化情况。

结果：在720p文本到视频和图像到视频生成任务上，SparSTAR保留了所有标记和细化尺度，实现了约1.6倍的端到端加速，同时VBench指标和配对输出重建保真度与稠密InfinityStar保持接近。

结论：实验表明重要键块会随尺度变化而移动，在每个目标尺度重新计算块选择比复用转移掩码更可靠，验证了SparSTAR方法在视频视觉自回归生成中的有效性。

原始摘要：InfinityStar extends visual autoregressive generation to video through a sequence of image and clip pyramids. Its changing scale and cross-clip context, however, leave late-scale attention costly and make sparse patterns reused from diffusion or image VAR models unreliable. We introduce SparSTAR, a training-free block-sparse attention method tailored to this setting. At each expensive scale and attention head, SparSTAR scores contiguous key blocks from the current query and key activations, retains required conditioning context, and executes the selected blocks through a forward-only sparse path. We analyze cross-scale consistency within a clip, pattern persistence across clip boundaries, and quality degradation as reuse spans increasingly distant scales. Across these analyses, important key blocks shift, showing that recomputing block selection at each target scale is more reliable than reusing a transferred mask. On 720p text-to-video and image-to-video generation, SparSTAR preserves every token and refinement scale while providing about a 1.6x end-to-end speedup and maintaining VBench and paired-output reconstruction fidelity close to dense InfinityStar.

### [4] [AdvFD: Boosting Visual Generation via Adversarial Fr'echet Distance Loss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11205)
*Mingju Gao,Jingkai Zhou,Kun Gai,Changqian Yu,Hao Tang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出对抗式弗雷歇距离（AdvFD），通过引入可学习的对抗表示与真实特征白化处理，解决生成器后训练中弗雷歇距离优化导致的"弗雷歇攻击"问题，在多种骨干网络和模型规模上一致提升了一步生成器的后训练效果。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有的Fréchet距离损失使用静态预训练特征空间，对真实与生成分布之间的差异提供不完整且固定的视角，导致直接优化Fréchet目标时出现"弗雷歇攻击"现象——目标指标持续改善但视觉质量和其他特征空间中的Fréchet对齐停滞或退化。

方法：提出AdvFD方法：1) 在静态Fréchet目标基础上，引入可学习的对抗表示，最大化真实与生成样本之间的Fréchet差异，而生成器在同一自适应特征空间中最小化该差异；2) 引入真实特征白化（real-feature whitening），归一化特征的尺度和协方差几何，防止对抗表示通过特征放大而平凡地增大目标，从而稳定min-max优化过程。

结果：大量实验表明，AdvFD在JiT和pMF两种骨干网络上、不同模型规模下，一致地提升了一步生成器后训练的性能。

结论：通过将静态特征空间与对抗学习的自适应表示相结合，并辅以白化归一化稳定训练，AdvFD有效克服了Fréchet攻击问题，为生成器后训练提供了更鲁棒的分布级优化目标。

原始摘要：Fréchet distance has recently emerged as an effective distribution-level objective for generator post-training, complementing the conventional sample-level diffusion and flow-matching losses. However, directly optimizing Fréchet objectives can cause Fréchet hacking. The target metrics keep improving, but visual quality and Fréchet alignment in other feature spaces may stagnate or deteriorate. We attribute this failure to the static pretrained feature spaces used by existing Fréchet losses. These feature spaces provide incomplete and fixed views of the differences between real and generated distributions. To address this limitation, we propose Adversarial Fréchet Distance (AdvFD), which complements the static representation targets in FD-Loss with a calibrated adversarially learned representation. AdvFD augments the original static Fréchet objective with a learnable representation that adversarially maximizes the Fréchet discrepancy between real and generated samples, while the generator minimizes the same discrepancy in the resulting adaptive feature space. To prevent the adversarial representation from trivially increasing the objective through feature amplification, we further introduce real-feature whitening, which normalizes its scale and covariance geometry and stabilizes the min--max optimization. Extensive experiments show that AdvFD consistently improves one-step generator post-training across both JiT and pMF backbones and across different model scales.

</details>
