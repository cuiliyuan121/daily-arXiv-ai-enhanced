### [1] [DensityKV: Density-Guided KV Cache Compression for Long Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27922)
*Wenqu Zhao,Xuemin Chi,Xin Zhang,Guoqing Ma,Baorun Li,Jianjie Fang,Peizhi Tang,Chen Gao,Wei Wu*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出DensityKV，一种无需训练的历史KV银行管理策略，通过捕捉每个注意力头中局部冗余并使用Soft-Riesz密度约束邻域密度增长，以限制重复历史积累，在保持有界历史存储的同时提升自回归视频生成的长程一致性和稳定性。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自回归视频扩散模型通过滑动窗口注意力实现流式生成，但每块生成内容会递归传播外观和运动误差。虽然历史KV记忆有助于维持长程一致性，但保留所有状态会导致历史档案无限增长且存在大量冗余覆盖。

方法：DensityKV为每个注意力头维护独立的token级KV银行，利用Soft-Riesz密度度量post-RoPE键（直接参数化注意力路由）的局部冗余，通过约束状态进入银行后的邻域密度增长来限制重复历史积累，同时保留每个生成块中的连贯状态。

结果：在三个自回归视频生成骨干网和多种生成长度上，在相同历史KV容量上界下，DensityKV显著提升了长程一致性和生成稳定性，并使得持久历史存储与展开（rollout）长度无关，保持有界。

结论：DensityKV通过基于密度感知的历史KV管理，在有限存储下有效解决了自回归视频生成中的误差传播和冗余积累问题，为长视频流式生成提供了一种高效且普适的解决方案。

原始摘要：Autoregressive video diffusion models enable streaming generation through sliding-window attention, but each generated block is conditioned on previously generated content, causing appearance and motion errors to propagate recursively over time. Historical key-value (KV) memory preserves earlier subject and scene states and helps maintain long-horizon consistency. However, retaining every generated state creates a historical archive that grows continuously with the rollout, while recurrent states repeatedly add redundant coverage. To address this problem, we propose DensityKV, a training-free historical KV bank management strategy. DensityKV maintains a separate token-level KV bank for each attention head and measures local redundancy among the post-RoPE keys that directly parameterize attention routing using Soft-Riesz density. By constraining neighborhood-density growth after states enter the bank, DensityKV limits repeated historical accumulation while preserving coherent states from each completed generation block. Experiments across three autoregressive video generation backbones and multiple generation lengths show that, at the same upper bound on historical KV capacity, DensityKV improves long-horizon consistency and generation stability while keeping persistent historical storage bounded independently of rollout length.

### [2] [Attribute Token Arithmetic: Disentangled and Continuous Semantic Control for Visual Autoregressive Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28082)
*Xindi Yang,Yicheng Wu,Cheng Zhang,Jianfei Cai,Tien-Tsin Wong*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出Attribute Token Arithmetic (ATA)方法，通过在预训练自回归潜空间中识别视觉属性的语义方向，实现无需重训练、单参考图像驱动的解耦连续属性控制，支持多属性组合编辑。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自回归文生图模型虽取得显著进展，但细粒度语义控制仍面临属性纠缠和文本-视觉表示不对齐的挑战。

方法：借鉴词嵌入向量算术性质，在预训练自回归潜空间中直接识别对应视觉属性（如衰老、肥胖、情绪）的语义方向，通过单张参考图像学习这些方向，生成时通过简单算术运算实现属性的连续调整和组合。

结果：实验表明ATA实现了身份保持、细粒度及多属性调整，在可控性、通用性和计算效率上优于现有自回归编辑基线。

结论：ATA为自回归视觉生成提供了一种无需重训练、高效且可解耦的属性控制新范式，代码已开源。

原始摘要：Autoregressive text-to-image generation has recently achieved remarkable progress, offering high-fidelity synthesis via a unified generative framework. However, fine-grained semantic control remains challenging due to the attribute entanglement and the misalignment between textual and fine-grained visual representations. In this paper, we introduce Attribute Token Arithmetic (ATA), a method that enables disentangled and continuous attribute control in visual autoregressive modelling. Inspired by the vector arithmetic property observed in word embeddings, ATA identifies semantic directions corresponding to visual attributes (e.g., aging, fatness, emotion) directly within the pretrained autoregressive latent space. These directions are learned from a single reference image, without model retraining or large-scale supervision. During generation, attributes can be continuously adjusted and compositionally combined through simple arithmetic operations with other attribute tokens. Extensive experiments demonstrate that ATA achieves identity-preserving, fine-grained, and multi-attribute adjustment, outperforming existing autoregressive editing baselines in controllability, generality, and computational efficiency. Our code will be available at https://github.com/Madaoer/ATA.

</details>


### [3] [How Far Can 5,500 Hours of Driving Take You? A Scaling Law Analysis of Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28404)
*Victor Besnier,Anh-Quan Cao,Elias Ramzi,Spyros Gidaris,Tuan-Hung Vu,Andrei Bursuc,Eloi Zablocki,Matthieu Cord*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文通过系统性的缩放定律研究，训练了从1M到9B参数的视频扩散模型（在最多5500小时驾驶数据上），发现验证损失随模型大小和训练暴露量遵循幂定律，并据此训练了9B参数的最大驾驶视频生成模型，在nuScenes上创下开源SOTA。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自动驾驶视频生成不同于网络规模的路线：驾驶数据采集成本高、受隐私限制、无法随意爬取，因此模型必须在固定语料库上充分发挥效能，需要通过缩放定律研究来指导训练预算分配。

方法：从零开始在驾驶数据上训练一系列视频扩散模型（1M到9B参数），在不同训练暴露量下进行系统实验，建立验证损失与模型规模、训练暴露量之间的幂定律关系，分析计算预算应分配给更长训练还是更大模型，并基于这些规律训练最终模型。

结果：发现损失随训练暴露量的改善远快于随模型规模的改善（更长训练是固定计算下提升模型最有效的方式）；但更大模型能达到更低渐近损失，在计算和数据充足时增大模型仍是最优策略。据此训练的9B参数模型成为驾驶数据上从零训练的最大视频扩散模型，在nuScenes上达到开源SOTA。

结论：缩放定律为自动驾驶视频生成提供了计算预算分配的系统性指导，验证了在数据受限场景下延长训练时间比增大模型更有效，但计算充足时模型规模仍是关键因素；9B模型证明了该策略的有效性，代码与数据将开源。

原始摘要：Video generation for autonomous driving cannot follow the web-scale route: driving data is expensive to collect, bound by privacy requirements, and cannot be scraped at will, so models must make the most of a fixed corpus. We present a systematic scaling-law study of video diffusion models trained from scratch on driving data: a family of models from 1M to 9B parameters, trained at different exposures on up to 5,500 hours of driving. Validation loss follows consistent power laws in both model size and training exposure, answering the questions that shape a training budget: whether compute is better spent on longer training or on a larger model, and whether more data is needed. Loss improves much faster with training exposure than with model size, making longer training the most effective way to improve a fixed model under limited compute. However, larger models continue to achieve lower asymptotic loss, so compute-optimal scaling still favors increasing model size when sufficient compute and data are available. Guided by these laws, we train a 9B-parameter model, to our knowledge the largest video diffusion model trained from scratch on driving data: it sets a new open-source state of the art for driving video generation, as measured on nuScenes. Our code and pretrained models are available at https://github.com/valeoai/VATIX. NATIX is separately releasing the underlying driving data in stages.

</details>


### [4] [LayerRecall: A State-Conditioned Memory Router for Long-Horizon Consistency in Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28460)
*Yixuan Ding,Jiahao Kong,Wei Huang,Ruijie Quan,Yi Yang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出LayerRecall，一种条件化层选择性记忆路由机制，通过将检索到的历史K/V状态仅注入骨干中记忆敏感层，实现长视频生成中的长程记忆恢复，并配合Cross-Horizon Prediction Matching（CHPM）监督策略减少对高质量长视频数据的依赖。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：自回归视频扩散模型在长视频生成中依赖近期缓存保持局部连续性，但会丢失历史线索，导致主体、物体、场景或属性重现时无法恢复。现有记忆机制仅暴露非局部历史，并未确保其有效使用。作者分析发现视频DiT不同层对当前、近期和远期上下文有不同偏好，因此长程记忆需要同时决定检索什么和在哪里使用。

方法：1) LayerRecall：一种当前条件化的层选择性记忆路由器，检索相关的历史K/V状态，仅注入骨干中特定记忆敏感层，其余层保持局部注意力；2) CHPM（Cross-Horizon Prediction Matching）：利用特权长上下文参考在预测空间监督有界记忆路由器，降低对稀缺高质量长视频数据和显式记忆分配标签的依赖。

结果：在100个多镜头评估提示上，LayerRecall在MemoBench和MovieBench上取得最佳总体结果，同时在VBench-Long上与骨干匹配。定性和定量分析显示，该方法实现记忆引导的自我修正，即初始不匹配的局部属性恢复到历史外观而不中断运动或场景结构；还展现了跨骨干可移植性和可忽略的推理开销。

结论：LayerRecall有效解决了自回归视频扩散中长程记忆缺失的关键问题，证明了"检索什么"和"在哪使用"的联合决策对长距离恢复至关重要，且其监督策略降低了数据需求，具有良好的实用性和扩展性。

原始摘要：Autoregressive video diffusion enables scalable long-video generation by producing chunks from a bounded recent context. While recency-based caching preserves local continuity, it evicts historical cues needed when subjects, objects, scenes, or attributes reappear. Existing memory mechanisms expose models to nonlocal history, but access alone does not ensure effective use. Our analysis reveals that video DiT layers exhibit distinct preferences for current, recent, and distant context, suggesting that long-range memory requires deciding both what to retrieve and where to use it. We introduce LayerRecall, a current-conditioned, layer-selective memory router that retrieves relevant historical K/V states and injects them only into backbone-specific memory-sensitive layers while preserving local attention elsewhere. To reduce reliance on scarce high-quality long-horizon videos and explicit memory-allocation labels, we further propose Cross-Horizon Prediction Matching (CHPM), which uses a privileged long-context reference to supervise the bounded-memory router in prediction space. Across 100 multi-shot evaluation prompts, LayerRecall achieves the best overall results on MemoBench and MovieBench while matching its backbone on VBench-Long, demonstrating stronger long-range recovery without sacrificing local continuity. Qualitative analyses further reveal memory-guided self-correction, whereby initially mismatched local attributes return to their historical appearance without resetting ongoing motion or scene structure. Additional analyses show cross-backbone portability and negligible inference overhead.

</details>
