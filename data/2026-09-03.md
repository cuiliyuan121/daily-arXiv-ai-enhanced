### [1] [RoGe: Novel View Synthesis via End-to-End Implicit Reconstruction and Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02847)
*Xiaolei Lang,Ze Kang,Zehao Huang,Naiyan Wang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：RoGe是一个端到端的重建与生成统一框架，消除重建与生成之间的显式桥梁（如渲染图像或3D表示），通过从稀疏输入视图构建隐式场景表征，并用目标相机光线查询得到逐视图几何特征，直接注入视频扩散模型作为条件，实现沿轨迹的时序一致视频合成。两模块联合训练，生成目标直接塑造其几何条件，在DL3DV上优于基于重建、生成及混合方法的基线。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：现有稀疏输入的新视角合成混合方法在重建与生成之间通过渲染图像或显式3D表示（如点图、3D高斯）作为桥梁，导致生成模块受限于有损且不完美的场景投影，继承了其误差；同时重建模块无法从生成中获得反馈来纠正这些误差。

方法：RoGe包含两个联合训练的模块：（1）前馈重建模型从稀疏视角构建隐式场景表征；（2）该隐式表征通过目标相机光线查询获得逐视角几何特征，不经过任何3D中间表示，直接注入视频扩散模型作为条件。重建目标和生成目标联合优化，使生成目标直接作用于几何条件的学习。

结果：在DL3DV数据集上，RoGe在图像级指标和视频级时序一致性方面均优于基于重建、基于生成和混合方法的基线。消融实验表明，光线查询的隐式特征优于原始重建token和渲染RGB作为条件，联合训练带来进一步增益。

结论：RoGe通过去除重建与生成之间的显式中间表示，采用光线查询的隐式几何特征作为扩散模型条件并联合训练，有效弥合了稀疏视图重建与生成之间的鸿沟，提升了场景漫游视频合成的质量和时序一致性。

原始摘要：Novel view synthesis from sparse inputs requires both geometric grounding from the observed views and generative priors of unobserved regions, motivating recent hybrid methods that combine reconstruction and generation. However, existing methods bridge the two with rendered images or explicit 3D representations such as point maps or 3D Gaussians. Generation is thus conditioned on a lossy and imperfect projection of the scene, inheriting its errors, and reconstruction receives no signal from generation to correct them. We present RoGe, an end-to-end unified reconstruction and generation framework that removes this explicit bridge. It targets roaming within a scene anchored by sparse views: given a few posed images and a camera trajectory, it synthesizes a temporally coherent video along that trajectory. From the sparse input views, RoGe builds an implicit scene representation with a feed-forward reconstruction model, and queries it with target camera rays to obtain per-view geometric features. These features are injected into a video diffusion model as conditioning, without any 3D intermediate. Both modules are trained jointly, so the generation objective directly shapes its own geometric conditioning. We conduct experiments on DL3DV, where RoGe outperforms reconstruction-based, generation-based, and hybrid baselines on image-level metrics and video-level temporal consistency. Ablations confirm that ray-queried implicit features outperform both raw reconstruction tokens and rendered RGB as conditioning, and that joint training brings further gains.

### [2] [SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886)
*Junchao Huang,Guian Fang,Shengju Qian,Xianghao Kong,Zhuoran Zhao,Wei Huang,Yihua Du,Zixin Zhang,Justin Cui,Yuchao Gu,Yukang Chen,Xinting Hu,Tianyu He,Shaoshuai Shi,Zhuotao Tian,Xin Wang,Mike Zheng Shou,Li Jiang*

主分类：cs.CV

一句话总结：本文提出SolarWM，一个完全开源的交互式视频世界模型基础框架，涵盖从数据处理到长时程推理的全流程。通过可重构的多源数据引擎和骨干网络自适应框架，统一了来自10个数据集的143万条视频片段，并基于Wan2.2、LTX-2.5和MiniMax-H3等骨干网络实例化了四个5B-33B规模的因果模型，使其仅经过5秒序列训练即可支持数分钟到数小时的实时交互。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：异构数据源（时间尺度、相机几何、视觉质量、运动、标注风格各异）与不同视频生成架构（表征和设计差异）之间的耦合问题导致训练监督不一致、结果难以复现和比较，亟需一个统一的完全开源基础框架来解决这一挑战。

方法：SolarWM包含两大部分：(1) 可重构多源数据引擎，将10个数据集的143万条规范片段转换为统一的逐帧对齐协议（涵盖视觉观测、度量相机几何、标题、质量元数据、选择决策和溯源），并解耦源处理与混合构建；(2) 骨干网络原生适配框架，在共享的相机条件、训练和推理接口下保持各骨干网络的原生表征和目标。采用统一的三阶段训练策略：双向适配、教师强制的自回归初始化和分布匹配蒸馏。

结果：基于Wan2.2、LTX-2.5和MiniMax-H3骨干网络实例化了四个5B-33B规模的因果模型，在仅5秒序列训练后即可实现从数分钟到数小时的滚动预测上的实时交互，并发布了全部数据、管道、配方、权重和框架。

结论：SolarWM通过解耦数据处理与模型适配，为交互式世界模型研究提供了一个可复现、可扩展的完全开源基础，解决了多源数据与异构骨干网络之间的耦合难题，验证了短序列训练实现长时程实时交互的可行性。

原始摘要：We introduce SolarWM, a fully open foundation for building interactive video world models from data preparation through long-horizon inference. Training across heterogeneous data sources and video backbones is challenging: datasets differ in temporal scale, camera geometry, visual quality, motion, and captioning styles, while video generators use distinct representations and architectures. Naive data mixing and model-specific implementations therefore produce inconsistent supervision and make results difficult to reproduce and compare. SolarWM addresses this coupling with a reconfigurable multi-source data engine and a backbone-native adaptation framework. The engine converts 1.43 million canonical clips from 10 datasets into a unified, frame-aligned contract covering visual observations, metric camera geometry, captions, quality metadata, selection decisions, and provenance, while decoupling source processing from mixture construction. Under shared camera-conditioning, training, and inference interfaces, we instantiate four 5B--33B models based on Wan2.2, LTX-2.5, and MiniMax-H3 while preserving their native representations and objectives. A unified three-stage recipe combines bidirectional adaptation, teacher-forced autoregressive initialization, and distribution matching distillation. The resulting causal models enable real-time interaction over rollouts ranging from minutes to hours after being trained on only 5s sequences. By releasing the resulting data, pipeline, recipes, weights, and framework, SolarWM provides a reproducible and extensible foundation for interactive world-model research.

### [3] [From Reweighting to Rewriting: Unlocking the Intervention Effects of Influential Samples in Training Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02771)
*Yuzhang Luo,Chenpeng Wang,Jianhui Chen,Liangming Pan*

主分类：cs.CL

一句话总结：本文提出"影响力引导的响应改写"方法，用影响函数识别干预目标后改写其响应（对齐或反对行为的监督），相比传统的加权干预能产生更强、更持久且双向的行为改变，揭示了影响函数的局部加权效应与所识别样本的干预潜力之间的差异。


<details>
  <summary>详细信息</summary>
研究动机：影响函数选择的样本在传统加权干预下相比随机选择优势有限，这引发了疑问：是有影响力的样本本身缺乏干预价值，还是加权方式未能充分发挥其行为杠杆作用？

方法：使用影响函数（IF）识别干预目标样本，在保持指令不变的前提下替换这些样本的响应为行为对齐或行为反对的监督信号，与传统的响应重加权方法在相同的IF选择样本上进行对比实验。

结果：在四个开源大语言模型上，以认知弃权（epistemic abstention）为主要测试场景，响应改写比重加权产生更强、更持久且双向的行为转变；重加权相同样本则效果微弱且不一致。影响函数选择的样本在改写任务上优于其他选择器，且行为变化集中在目标相关的行为上；该定性对比同样适用于安全拒绝场景。

结论：影响估计捕获的局部重加权效应，与它们所识别样本的更广泛干预潜力是不同的。这激励了对训练数据归因方法进行"干预感知"的评估。

原始摘要：Training data attribution (TDA) aims to identify training examples that shape model behavior, but its intervention value depends on both which examples are selected and how they are modified. Influence functions (IF) estimate behavioral changes under infinitesimal reweighting, yet IF-selected examples often show limited advantages over random selection under conventional weight-based interventions. This raises the question of whether influential examples lack intervention value or whether reweighting fails to realize their behavioral leverage.We introduce influence-guided response rewriting, which uses IF to identify intervention targets and replaces their responses with behavior-aligned or behavior-opposed supervision while keeping instructions fixed. Across four open-weight LLMs, we compare rewriting and reweighting on the same influence-selected examples using epistemic abstention as our primary testbed. Response rewriting produces stronger, more persistent, and bidirectional behavioral shifts, while reweighting the same examples yields weak and inconsistent effects. Further analyses show that influence-selected examples provide greater rewriting leverage than alternative selectors, with changes remaining concentrated on target-relevant behaviors. The same qualitative contrast extends to safety refusal. These results distinguish the local reweighting effects captured by influence estimates from the broader intervention leverage of the examples they identify, motivating intervention-aware evaluation of TDA methods.

</details>
