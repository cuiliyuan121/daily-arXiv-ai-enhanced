### [10] [Position: Natural Language Should Not Fully Replace Formal Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20432)
*Eitan Wagner,Elisha Rosensweig,Omri Abend*

主分类：cs.CL

一句话总结：本文论证自然语言虽在低特异性任务中表现出色，但在高严格要求的任务上，形式语言更具优势，并提出了"特异性交叉定理"，主张开发混合系统以结合两者优势。

研究动机：针对当前认为自然语言可完全替代形式语言（如编程语言）的观点，作者指出这一立场忽略了自然语言在开放语境中倾向于欠指定的根本特性，因此需要重新审视两者之间的关系。

方法：提出基于信息论的"任务特异性"定义，并证明"特异性交叉定理"；通过图像生成、代码合成、音频制作等多模态案例进行对比分析。

结果：自然语言在低特异性任务中高效且灵活，而形式语言在严格要求的任务中成本更低、精度更高。两者存在明确的分工阈值。

结论：自然语言与形式语言应被视为互补工具，未来应发展混合系统，使用户能够根据任务特异性在两者之间灵活切换。

原始摘要：Recent advances in large language models and their widespread adoption have prompted claims that natural language could entirely replace formal languages, such as programming languages for software design. In this position paper, we argue that this perspective overlooks fundamental linguistic properties of natural language, specifically that it is optimized for underspecification in open-ended contexts. We introduce a formal framework centered on *task specificity*, defining it as the information-theoretic reduction of uncertainty in an output space -- such as all possible images -- given a user's specific requirements. We prove a *specificity crossover theorem*, showing the existence of a threshold beyond which the cost to express formal requirements into natural language exceeds the cost of direct formal specification. By analyzing case studies across modalities, such as image generation, code synthesis, and audio production, we demonstrate that natural language excels at low specificity tasks, while formal languages are advantageous on tasks with stricter requirements. We conclude that natural and formal languages are complementary tools and advocate the development of hybrid systems that allow users to move across the specificity spectrum.
